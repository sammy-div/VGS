Our story

Built from the operations floor up

Vatous began with a simple observation: most African businesses don't fail for lack of ambition — they lose time, money and momentum to broken processes, disconnected tools and manual work that quietly compounds.

We set out to fix that with a different kind of firm — one that combines the engineering depth of a product studio, the rigour of a consultancy, and a deep understanding of how organizations actually operate on the ground.
