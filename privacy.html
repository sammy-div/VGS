Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This policy explains what information Vatous Global Solutions collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain and practical.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and operate this website:

  • Details you submit through our forms — such as your name, email, company and message.
  • Newsletter email addresses, when you subscribe.
  • Basic, aggregated analytics about how pages are used.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to enquiries, process job applications, send you material you asked for, and improve our website. We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored securely in our Supabase database. Access is restricted to authorised team members and protected by row-level security and authentication.

Data retention

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were provided, or as required by law, after which they are deleted.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies, primarily for essential functionality and privacy-respecting analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
