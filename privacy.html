This notice explains, in plain language, how Vatous Global Solutions handles information you share through this website.

1. Information we receive

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, organization, project details and the contents of messages submitted through our contact forms. If you subscribe to updates, we receive the email address you submit.

Basic technical information, such as browser type, device details and website activity, may be processed by hosting, security or analytics services used to operate and improve the site.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, understand project needs, provide requested information, maintain site security and improve our services. We may retain relevant correspondence so we can follow up accurately.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us host the site, deliver email, manage enquiries or maintain security. We expect those providers to handle information in line with their service obligations and applicable law.

We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect our rights, users or systems.

4. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose collected, unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you have provided, or to stop sending optional updates. Some records may need to be retained where there is a legal or operational reason to do so. This website may use essential local storage for preferences, such as your cookie-consent choice.

6. Third-party services and links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and this notice does not govern their services. Please review their policies before submitting information to them.

7. Contact us

For questions or requests about this notice, contact hello@vatous.ng or use our contact page.

This notice describes our website practices and is not a statement about separate products or services that may provide their own notices.
