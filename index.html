03 / Approach

Understand first. Build second.

Every engagement is shaped around the problem in front of us. The order below is deliberate.

01

02
Shape
→
03
Build

01 Understand the problem Sit with the people doing the work. Find where it actually breaks, not where the brief says it does.
02 Shape the approach Agree what to build, what to leave alone and how we will know it worked.
03 Design and build Design, engineering and content move together so the result holds up in real use.
04 Hand over and improve Document it, train the team and stay close while the system settles into daily work.
