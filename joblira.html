Joblira

A work and opportunity platform that helps people turn their skills into real work, earn money, build experience, and develop a professional track record over time.

Explore Joblira Read the case study
What it does
Connects people looking for work with opportunities that match their abilities.
Who it serves
Job seekers building a career, and employers building a team.
The problem
Finding the right opportunity is fragmented, slow and often unclear for both sides.
Who runs it
Developed and operated by Vatous Global Solutions.
02 / The problem

Finding the right opportunity shouldn't feel like endless searching.

People have abilities worth demonstrating, even if they are new to professional work or have limited experience. Joblira is designed to bridge that gap.

01 Skills People have abilities worth demonstrating, even if they are new to professional work or have limited experience.
02 Real tasks Earners can discover and apply for tasks that match their skills and the opportunities available on the platform.
03 Earnings and experience Earners undertake agreed tasks and receive payment in accordance with the platform's applicable terms and completion requirements.
04 Professional profile and portfolio Completed work can contribute to a stronger profile, portfolio, and record of experience.
05 Bigger opportunities As Earners develop relevant experience and demonstrate their capabilities, they can use their growing track record to pursue more substantial tasks, projects, and other career opportunities.

04 / Client experience

Getting work done.

Clients can connect with people whose abilities are relevant to their tasks or projects.

01 Find people with relevant skills Clients can connect with Earners whose abilities are relevant to their tasks or projects.
02 Get work done Clients can post tasks, describe their requirements, and engage suitable people to complete the agreed work.
03 Discover capability through work Profiles, available portfolios, completed tasks, and experience records can help evaluate candidates where these features are supported by the product.
04 Support different kinds of Clients Joblira serves both individual Clients and business or company Clients.
05 / Principles

Product principles, not just product launch.

01 Separate identity A product keeps its own name, audience and voice. Vatous is the company behind it, not a badge on top of it.
02 Built by the same team Design, engineering and content decisions sit together, which is why the product feels like one thing.
03 Run after launch A product is only finished when it is stable, supported and still useful months later.
