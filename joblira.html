Case study

Building Joblira

From an employment challenge to a scalable digital platform.

The challenge

The employment ecosystem is fragmented. Job seekers search across many platforms and compete for attention; employers struggle to reach the right people efficiently. The challenge was to bring opportunities and people closer together — simply, accessibly and at scale.

Our approach

We approached Joblira as a scalable digital product — considering user needs and journeys, opportunity discovery, talent visibility, employer interaction, information architecture, responsive design and a technology foundation that could evolve with its users.

The outcome

Joblira became a demonstration of how Vatous approaches technology: start with a real problem, understand the people affected, design around their needs, and build technology that can grow with the solution.
